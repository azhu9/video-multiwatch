Multiwatch — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

Multiwatch is a Chrome extension for watching several video tabs at once. It does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, browsing history, or any other information.

What the extension does

  • It only interacts with tabs you explicitly choose to track, by clicking its toolbar icon or using the "Track this tab in Multiwatch" right-click menu item on that tab.
  • The list of tabs you're tracking, and which one currently has audio, is stored locally in Chrome's session storage (chrome.storage.session). This data never leaves your browser, is never synced to any account or server, and is cleared automatically when you close your browser.
  • Multiwatch makes no network requests of any kind. It does not call any external server, API, or analytics service. Nothing is ever sent anywhere.

Permissions

The permissions Multiwatch requests (tabs, tabCapture, activeTab, storage, contextMenus, scripting) are used solely to capture and display video from tabs you explicitly track, and to manage those specific tabs (e.g. pausing a tracked tab's video, or preventing Chrome from discarding it in the background). None of these permissions are used to read, collect, or transmit your data anywhere.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the update will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
